\section{Conclusion}

We presented \textsc{ZwerGe-UI}, a frozen-backbone retrofit that exposes and exploits the coordinate serialization bottleneck in GUI agents: instruction-conditioned spatial posteriors peak at intermediate transformer layers, while later layers increasingly specialize in coordinate serialization rather than localization refinement.
A training-free, GT-free confidence gate combines the fused intermediate-layer point with the native decoder at the cost of a single forward pass.
Across two backbone families and six benchmarks, all four retrofitted agents improve over their native baselines, and the improvement is statistically significant under a paired test ($p{<}0.001$).
The gains are modest by design: the retrofit's purpose is to validate the bottleneck hypothesis rather than chase leaderboard scores, and the heads train on far less data than the backbone, leaving clear room for improvement as GUI agents scale.
